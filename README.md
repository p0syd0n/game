# game

a pacman-style multiplayer game.

Object: collect points. 

Collect green dots (juice) in order to gain juice. Bring your juice to the refgidgerator  (blue) in order to deposit your juice. If you reach 0 lives with undeposited juice, you lose all your juice (it spills all over your current tile, and someone else may pick it  up)


and it only took 10 hours!
